Featured work

Counterfactual Experience Replay via Structural Causal Models In revision

Causal RL framework for financial decision-making under non-stationarity. Learns a causal graph over market features and generates counterfactual transitions via Pearl's do-calculus, paired with a variance-penalized policy objective. Evaluated across 30 assets and multiple market regimes, including the COVID-19 crash and the 2022 bear market.

AGAKA: Hierarchical Multi-Agent Framework for Maritime Intelligence In preparation

Developed at CAIR, DRDO. A decision agent accepts or rejects incoming documents using a learned, reliability-adaptive threshold; feedback loops update scoring weights and acquisition priorities as source reliability shifts over time.

Benchmarking against static-threshold baselines across maritime intelligence feeds
